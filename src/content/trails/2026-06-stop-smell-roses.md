## Summary

A big thank you to everyone who attended this year's Stop and Smell the Roses. It was a great time spent with great company. Please share your photos on the club's Facebook page so we can all relive the experience.

## Highlights

- **Leavitt Lake overnight** at 10,000 feet with campfire and fishing
- **Fishing contest** at Sand Flat Campground for the biggest catch
- **Niagara Rim Trail** — rated 6, moderate with some challenging obstacles (bypasses available)
- Potluck breakfasts and taco dinner

## Base Camp

Sand Flat Campground, off Highway 108 towards Sonora Pass on Clark Fork Road.
