## About

Hi Desert Round-Up is Cal4Wheel's annual Memorial Day weekend event held at the Slash X Ranch near Barstow. This long-running event features a variety of trail runs for all skill levels, from beginner-friendly SUV runs to challenging obstacle courses.

Beyond the trails, HDR offers games, camping, evening BBQ, and one of Cal4Wheel's famous raffles. It's a great kickoff to summer wheeling season with fellow 4x4 enthusiasts.

Preregistration typically includes a free event T-shirt. Sign-ups for runs are available at the event in the registration area.
