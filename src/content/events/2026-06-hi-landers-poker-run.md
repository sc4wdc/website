## About

Join us for the Hi-Landers Poker Run on the Rubicon Trail! This is a fun, social event where participants collect playing cards at checkpoints along the route. At the end, the best poker hand wins prizes.

The route runs from Loon Lake to Wentworth Springs, covering some of the Rubicon's scenic terrain at a moderate pace. It's a great way to experience this legendary trail with a group of fellow 4x4 enthusiasts.
