## 🧸 About the Toy Run

Join us for our Annual Toy Run! On December 12, 2026, we'll gather at Tractor Supply in Watsonville and deliver toys together to Jacob's Heart Organization.
Jacob’s Heart Children’s Cancer Support Services exists to improve the quality of life for children with cancer and support their families in the challenges they face.

Gifts are for children ages 1-18.

Please **do not wrap** the toys. Keep them in their original boxes.

### 📍 Alternative Drop-off Locations

Don't fret if you can't make it to the Toy Run. You can drop off your toys directly at Jacob's Heart location or in one of three Toy Boxes:
* C&N Tractors, 496 Salinas Rd, Watsonville
* Aptos Landscape Supply, 5035 Freedom Blvd, Aptos
* Four Corners Market, 1003 Amesti Rd, Watsonville
