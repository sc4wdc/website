## Location

## About

Come out to this beautiful desert area featuring amazing sunrises, sunsets, and stargazing! Panamint Valley Days is a Cal4Wheel fundraiser supporting the association's operations and conservation efforts.

The event offers a great variety of runs ranging from scenic and historic SUV routes for stock vehicles to hardcore four-wheeling for built rigs. The Gear Grinders from Ridgecrest lead the runs and can help you choose the right one for your vehicle.

## Event Highlights

- **Runs Friday, Saturday & Sunday** – Something for every skill level
- **Saturday night raffle** – Great prizes including BFG tires, Warn winch, and more
- **Camping** – Thursday through Sunday
- **"Escape from Death Valley" add-on** – Retrace the path of the 49ers on a multi-day run leading into the event

## Pricing (based on prior years)

- **Members**: $75/vehicle
- **Non-members**: $100/vehicle
- Includes dash plaque, runs Friday & Saturday, camping
- Escape from Death Valley add-on: $65

## What to Bring

- Camping gear for desert conditions
- Campfire permit (may be required – check closer to event date)
- Layers for cool desert nights
- Camera for the incredible sunrises and sunsets!
