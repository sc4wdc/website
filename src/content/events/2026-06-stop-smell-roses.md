## About

Take a break from the technical stuff and join us for "Stop and Smell the Roses" – a laid-back overland adventure in the beautiful Sierra Nevada. This trip is all about enjoying the journey: scenic forest roads, easy trails, good fishing, and quality time camping with fellow club members.

Perfect for those who want to get out and explore without the stress of extreme obstacles.
