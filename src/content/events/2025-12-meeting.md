## 📋 About Our Monthly Meetings

Join us for our monthly club meeting! These gatherings are the heart of our club, where members come together to share stories, plan adventures, and connect with fellow off-road enthusiasts.

### What to Expect

- **Club Business** – Updates on club activities, announcements, and important news
- **Trip Planning** – Discuss upcoming runs, trail conditions, and coordinate logistics
- **Social Time** – Meet other members, swap stories, and build lasting friendships
- **Guest Speakers** – Occasional presentations on topics of interest to our community

### 🍕 Location Details

We meet at Round Table Pizza in Watsonville. Feel free to arrive early to grab food and drinks before the meeting starts. Orders are placed individually.

### 👋 Everyone is Welcome

Whether you're a long-time member, new to the club, or just curious about what we do, you're welcome to join us! It's a great opportunity to learn more about the club and meet the community.
