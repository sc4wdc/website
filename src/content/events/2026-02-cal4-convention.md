## About

Join Cal4Wheel in celebrating their 67th anniversary at the annual Convention! This is the organization's premier gathering, bringing together clubs, vendors, and 4x4 enthusiasts from across California.

The weekend includes:

- **Seminars and workshops** on various 4x4 topics
- **Vendor displays** featuring the latest gear and accessories
- **General Assembly** meetings
- **Saturday night Awards Banquet** honoring clubs and members
- **Raffle** with great prizes
- **Life Member Breakfast** (Saturday morning, for Cal4Wheel life members)

## Registration

Individual registration is $100 per person, which includes:
- Weekend activities
- Dash plaque
- One raffle ticket
- Saturday night awards banquet

Event merchandise (T-shirts, hoodies) available as add-ons.
