## About

Join us for an unforgettable adventure in Moab, Utah – the off-road capital of the world! This trip combines rock crawling on legendary slickrock trails with overland exploration through stunning red rock canyon country.

We'll tackle iconic trails like Poison Spider Mesa and Cliff Hanger, with an optional overnight trip on the famous White Rim Trail for those wanting to extend the adventure.
