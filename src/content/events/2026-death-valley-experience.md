## About

Don’t miss Cal4Wheel’s Death Valley Experience on April 2-5, 2025 in Death Valley National Park and adjacent public lands.  Each day will feature a choice of narrated, guided runs to some famous, some infamous and some secretive points of interest.  Each evening will feature informative workshops and seminars about the park’s history and desert ecosystems.  DVE2025 headquarters will be based in Stovepipe Wells, CA.  Registration includes trail runs, delicious box lunches, an event shirt and the evening programs.

In the summer of 2022 and again in 2023, Death Valley was inundated by severe rain from summer monsoons and from Hurricane Hilary.  As a result, many Park roads and trails were closed due to storm damage which caused the partial curtailment of our Death Valley Experience 2024.  Happily, most of the roads have reopened, but some popular routes may remain closed.  Subject to change, runs are scheduled to Keane Wonder Mine, Chloride City, Chloride Cliffs, Butte Valley, Barker Ranch, Echo Canyon, Charcoal Kilns, Aguereberry Point, Skidoo, Darwin Falls, Saline Valley Warm Springs, Saratoga Springs, Tecopa Hot Springs, Titus Canyon, The Racetrack, Gold Point, NV, and Rhyolite, NV.
