## Planned Route

## About

Join us for a relaxed overland adventure through the Sierra Nevada! This multi-day trip follows scenic forest roads from Meadow Lake to Truckee, with plenty of time to explore, camp, and enjoy the remote wilderness.

This is an easy, beginner-friendly trip – perfect for stock vehicles and those new to overlanding.

## Trip Details

|---|---|
| **Trip Type** | Overland (forest roads, exploring, camping) |

## Vehicle Requirements

- **Minimum** – 2WD (stock vehicles welcome!)
- **Recommended** – No upgrades needed; stock vehicles are perfectly suited

## What to Bring

- **Camping gear** – Tent, sleeping bag, etc. for 4 nights
- **Food and water** – Plan for the full trip; this is a very remote location
- Cooler with provisions
- Basic tools and spare tire
- First aid kit
- Maps/GPS (cell service will be limited)
