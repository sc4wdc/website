## About

Annual Molina Ghost Run, hosted by the Central District, has been traditionally held at the Clear Creek Management Area near Coalinga. The area is currently under a closure order by the Bureau of Land Management, so the event is being held at the Hollister Hills SVRA instead. It is six miles west of Hollister in San Benito County.

The entire SVRA Off Road Park is used for the event, so don't miss out on this fun place to wheel!

Runs include:

* Group 1 Hard: Requires front and rear lockers and 35" tires. Hard trails and obstacles.
* Group 2 Moderate: Locker recommended, medium trails with options to fit participants' capabilities.
* Group 3 Easy Beginner/SUV Run: Suitable for first-timers and SUVs.
* Group 4 Side-by-Side: Options to fit participants' capabilities.
* Group 5 Tank Trap Extreme: For the extreme off-roader we are giving you a chance to be led on a run through the infamous Tank Trap by Clubfoot and his hand-picked team for $100 members/$130 non-members per vehicle.
