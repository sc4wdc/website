## Date

July 4th, 2026

## Schedule

- **9:00–9:30 AM** – Arrive, check in, and decorate your vehicle
- **10:30 AM** – Must be staged and ready
- **11:00 AM** – Parade starts

## Location

**Yellow Lot** on Carbonero Way, Scotts Valley, CA — the last parking lot on the right side. We are **Group #51**. Check in at registration and let them know you are with the Santa Cruz Four Wheel Drive Club.

## About

Join us for the annual 4th of July Parade in Scotts Valley! Deck out your rig with flags and patriotic decorations and roll through town with fellow club members to celebrate Independence Day.

This is a great family-friendly event and a fun way to show off our rigs to the community.

## Important Details

- **Limited to 30 vehicles**
- **Signed waiver form required** – one per vehicle, regardless of the number of passengers. Bring your signed form to check-in and hand it in under the name "Santa Cruz Four Wheel Drive Club." Extra forms will be available on site.

## Questions?

Contact Trail Leader Jason Stevens or reach out at our monthly club meetings.
