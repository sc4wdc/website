## Planned Route

## About

Explore the stunning Lakes Basin and Sierra Buttes area! This trip offers a mix of scenic forest roads and rock crawling opportunities, with routes ranging from easy cruising to challenging technical terrain.

The Lakes Basin is one of the Sierra Nevada's hidden gems, featuring alpine lakes, granite peaks, and incredible views of the Sierra Buttes.
