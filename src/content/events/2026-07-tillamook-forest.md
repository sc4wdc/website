## Dates

July 15-19, 2026

## Meeting Location

Tillamook, OR

## About

Join us for a multi-day adventure in Oregon's Tillamook State Forest! This Pacific Northwest gem offers rugged mountain trails, rocky climbs, and lush forested paths – plus the Oregon coast and sand dunes are just a short drive away.

The forest features trails for all skill levels, from scenic beginner routes like Cedar Tree Trail to challenging terrain like Firebreak 5 and Powerline Trail with steep climbs and technical sections.

## Trip Highlights

- **Diverse terrain** – Rocky climbs, forested trails, and optional dune riding nearby
- **Camping** – Primitive and developed camping available (Browns Camp has facilities)
- **Coastal access** – Explore the Oregon Dunes and Pacific beaches
- **Nearby attractions** – Tillamook Creamery, Cape Lookout State Park, Three Capes Scenic Route

## What to Bring

- Recovery gear (winch, tow straps, D-rings, traction boards)
- Camping supplies for 4 nights
- **Shovel** – Required by Tillamook State Forest
- Trail maps (cell service is unreliable)
- Spare tire and basic tools
- Layers for Pacific Northwest weather

## Vehicle Requirements

4x4 with good clearance recommended. Stock vehicles can handle easier trails, but some routes are more technical.
