## Dates

July 15-19, 2026 (4 nights)

## About

Tillamook State Forest offers rocky climbs, lush forest trails, and optional coastal dune riding a short drive away. Nearby Sand Lake Recreation Area (~45 min from Tillamook) is great for a dune day. Astoria (~1.5 hrs north) is worth a side trip — especially if you're a fan of The Goonies, Short Circuit, or Kindergarten Cop.

## Itinerary

- **07/15 (Wed)** – Meet & greet in Tillamook; easy warm-up trails if time allows
- **07/16 (Thu)** – Cedar Tree (Jeep BOH #13) + easier forest trails
- **07/17 (Fri)** – Firebreak 5 (Jeep BOH #14) — hard day; lockers, 35" tires & winch recommended; early start
- **07/18 (Sat)** – Sand Lake Dunes (weather permitting)
- **07/19 (Sun)** – Departure

## Lodging

### Camping (closest to trails)

- **Brown's Camp OHV Campground** – Trailhead access, vault toilets, water; no hookups. OHV permit required for overnight stay. Tent / self-contained / toy hauler.
- **Trask River RV Park** (26905 Trask River Rd, Tillamook) – Full hookups, ~30 min to Brown's Camp; cabins & tiny houses available.

### Hotels (Tillamook area)

- **Ashley Inn of Tillamook** – Pool, breakfast (~15–20 min from Trask River)
- **Tillamook Inn** (Main St) – Budget-friendly, near the Creamery
- **Inn at Cape Kiwanda** (Pacific City) – Beach town, ~40 min from Tillamook

## Getting There

~12 hours from Santa Cruz — plan one long day or two shorter ones.

## Required Gear & Permits

- **Oregon ATV Operating Permit** – $10 / 2 years; required on OHV trails and dunes even for plated rigs ([purchase here](https://store.oregonstateparks.org)). CA reciprocity applies; WA/ID/UT/AZ do not.
- **Fire season (Tillamook forest):** shovel (blade ≥8", handle ≥26"), 2.5 lb+ ABC extinguisher
- **Sand Lake:** OHV permit + 9-ft red/orange whip flag
- Tow points & recovery gear
- Offline trail maps (cell service is unreliable)
- Spare tire and basic tools
- Layers for Pacific Northwest weather

## Vehicle Requirements

4x4 with good clearance recommended. Stock vehicles can handle easier trails, but some routes are more technical. Firebreak 5 requires lockers, 35" tires, and a winch.
