## Last One Standing

It's time for our annual fundraising event at Hollister Hills SVRA! This is our way of giving back to the public lands we enjoy and ensuring they remain open and accessible for future generations.

## Why We Do This

As a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization, one of our core missions is keeping public lands open. By volunteering our time to maintain trails, we:

- Demonstrate responsible land stewardship
- Build positive relationships with land managers
- Ensure continued access to trails
- Improve trail conditions for everyone
- Set a positive example for other users

## What We'll Be Doing

The "Last One Standing" is a family fun event that pits drivers of stock and modified 4x4 vehicles against each other and the clock to achieve the lowest time in several challenging off-road courses. The event is open to the public, limited to the first 30 registered contestants.
