It's time for our annual fundraising event at Hollister Hills SVRA!

The "Last One Standing" is a **family fun** event that pits drivers of stock and modified 4x4 vehicles against each other and the clock to achieve the lowest time in several challenging off-road courses.

The event is open to the public, limited to the first 30 registered contestants.

This is our way of giving back to the public lands we enjoy and ensuring they remain open and accessible for future generations.

## Pre-Register to Lock In Your Spot

Think your rig has what it takes? Put your 4x4 and driving skills to the ultimate test! Spots are limited to the first 30 registered contestants - don't wait.

### Classes

- **Sportsman**: up to 37" tires & light modifications
- **Unlimited**: 37"+ tires & heavily modified vehicles
- **Side-By-Side**: all side-by-side vehicles welcome

### How to Register

Email [los@sc4wdc.com](mailto:los@sc4wdc.com) with your rig details and the class you want to enter.

Bring a friend or family member! One of the events will include driving blindfolded — you'll need someone you trust to spot you and your rig safely through mud and obstacles.

## Schedule

- **9:00 AM** - Registration
- **10:00 AM** - Start of the Event
- **approx. 3:00 PM** - End of the Event

## Event Day Info

Open to public - spectators encouraged and free! (Park entrance fee required)

The club will provide hot dogs, drinks, and snacks.

After the event, we will hold a Raffle.

## Why We Do This

As a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization, one of our core missions is keeping public lands open. By volunteering our time to maintain trails, we:

- Demonstrate responsible land stewardship
- Build positive relationships with land managers
- Ensure continued access to trails
- Improve trail conditions for everyone
- Set a positive example for other users

All proceeds will be donated to organizations that keep OHV lands open.
