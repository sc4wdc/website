## About

Sierra Trek is Cal4Wheel's premier event in California's beautiful Tahoe National Forest. This family-oriented event offers something for everyone – from stock 4x4s on guided historic tours to fully built rock crawlers tackling the legendary Fordyce Creek Trail.

The weekend features great food (meals included!), a world-class vendor show, Cornhole Tournament, live band at the campfire, and plenty of time to make new friends and catch up with old ones.

## Run Options

**For Stock Vehicles:**
- Wednesday/Thursday Overnight
- Saturday Historic Run
- Outer Limits Run
- Signal Peak Run

**For Modified Vehicles:**
- Intermediate runs with beautiful scenery and moderate challenge
- Signal Peak Run

**Fordyce Creek Trail (Extreme):**
- Extremely technical and challenging
- Requires: 35" tires minimum, at least one locker
- Recommended: Larger tires, two lockers, crawler gearing

## What's Included

Registration includes:
- Thursday Dinner
- Friday & Saturday Breakfast and Dinner
- Sunday Breakfast
- Access to all camp activities, vendor show, and entertainment

## Important Notes

- **Wash your vehicle** before the event – USFS requests vehicles be washed to prevent spread of noxious weeds like yellow star thistle
- **Bring spare parts** – Fordyce is demanding; volunteers can help but can't carry parts for every vehicle
- **Plan your route in** – Large RVs and trailers can make it, but use caution on forest roads
