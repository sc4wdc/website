## Welcome to Santa Cruz 4WDC

The Santa Cruz 4 Wheel Drive Club is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization dedicated to keeping public lands open for everyone to enjoy. We're a passionate community of off-road enthusiasts who believe in responsible recreation and giving back to our community.

## Our Mission

We raise money to keep public lands open and help local non-profit organizations. Through our efforts, we:

- **Preserve Access**: Work with land managers to maintain and improve access to public trails
- **Trail Maintenance**: Organize work parties to maintain and restore trails
- **Community Support**: Partner with local non-profits to support important causes
- **Education**: Promote responsible off-roading and Leave No Trace principles
- **Fellowship**: Build a community of like-minded outdoor enthusiasts

## What We Do

### Trail Rides and Adventures

We organize monthly trail rides to explore the beautiful backcountry of California. From scenic coastal trails to challenging mountain passes, there's something for everyone regardless of skill level.

### Community Service

Our club actively participates in trail maintenance, habitat restoration, and supporting local charities. We believe in leaving our trails better than we found them.

### Education and Safety

We provide resources and training for safe and responsible off-roading. Our experienced members share their knowledge about vehicle preparation, recovery techniques, and trail etiquette.

## Our History

Founded by a group of passionate off-road enthusiasts in the Santa Cruz area, our club has grown into a vibrant community dedicated to preserving our natural heritage while enjoying the great outdoors.

## Get Involved

Whether you're a seasoned off-roader or just getting started, we welcome you to join us! Attend one of our monthly meetings to learn more about upcoming events, trail conditions, and how you can get involved.

**Everyone is welcome** - from stock SUVs to built-up rigs, what matters most is your enthusiasm for the outdoors and respect for the land.
