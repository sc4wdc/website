## About Our Board

The Board of Directors oversees club operations, organizes events, manages finances, and ensures we stay true to our mission of keeping public lands open. Board members are elected by club members and serve on a voluntary basis.

## Interested in Getting Involved?

Club leadership positions are open to active members who want to make a difference. Board elections are held biennial. If you're interested in a leadership role, we encourage you to attend meetings and get involved with club activities!
