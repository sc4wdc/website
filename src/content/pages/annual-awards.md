## About the Awards

Annual awards are presented at our year-end banquet to recognize members who have gone above and beyond. Categories range from mechanical prowess (Best Wrench) to community spirit (Helping Hand), and we also honor the trail that defined the year. Winners are nominated and voted on by the membership.
